\normalsize \textbf{Academic Integrity Declaration}\\
\end{center}

\noindent
We hereby declare that we have prepared all parts of this thesis independently and
have not used any sources or aids other than those specified in the thesis, and that
the thesis has not been submitted in the same or a similar form in any other
examination. All verbatim or in-sentence copies and quotations, as well as all
sections that were designed, written and/or edited with the help of AI-based tools,
have been identified and verified.

\vspace{1.0em}

\noindent
\textbf{Information on the use of AI-based tools}

\vspace{0.3em}

\noindent
In the appendix of our work, we have listed all AI-based resources. These are
identified with product names and formulated inputs (prompts) in an AI directory. We
assure that we have not used any AI-based tools whose use has been explicitly
excluded in writing by the examiner. We understand that the use of texts or other
content and products generated by AI-based tools does not guarantee their quality. We
are fully responsible for the adoption of any machine-generated passages used by us
and bear the responsibility for any erroneous or distorted content, faulty references,
violations of data protection and copyright law or plagiarism generated by the AI. We
also affirm that our creative influence predominates in the present work.
